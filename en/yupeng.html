Yupeng dry-docking

Solvent-free protection for the hull, waterline and cargo holds.

All customer projects

Dalian Maritime University’s ocean-going training vessel Yupeng completed dry-dock coating work using DW G50 solvent-free marine coating across large hull surfaces and confined cargo-hold areas.

01

The project challenge

A multifunctional vessel serving teaching, research and cargo operations required corrosion protection and abrasion resistance across different environments. Large external surfaces and confined cargo holds needed coordinated application and technical support.

02

The coating approach

The project used G50’s high-solids, solvent-free modified resin system for high-build application. Dowill’s technical team worked on site with the shipyard, supporting application and process control throughout the project.

Project photography

From preparation to completion

Project records describe completed coating work across the outer hull, waterline and cargo holds, with the vessel returning to service as scheduled. Low solvent emissions and reduced odor supported the application environment.
